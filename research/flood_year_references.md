# Historical Flood-Year References

## Primary Source

Kerala State Planning Board (2019).

Final Report of the Committee to Examine the
Causes of Repeated Extreme Heavy Rainfall Events,
Subsequent Floods and Landslides and to Recommend
Appropriate Policy Response.

Reference: Table 5.7, Page 123.

URL:
https://spb.kerala.gov.in/sites/default/files/inline-files/Final_Report.pdf

## Selected Historical Flood Years

The following years are documented in Table 5.7:

- 1924
- 1958
- 1961
- 1992
- 2007
- 2013

## Labelling Convention

1 = Year listed in the selected major-flood reference.

0 = Year not listed in the selected reference.

## Important Limitation

The reference lists selected major flood events.
It does not establish that all other years were
completely free from flooding.

Therefore, the target variable represents
documented major-flood years rather than
exhaustive flood occurrence.
